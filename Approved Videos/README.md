# Approved Videos

Keep your finished, approved edits here. These videos also serve as examples of the editing style you want future videos to follow.

## What each folder holds (tanmay branch)

| File or folder | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `<slug>-final.mp4`, `<slug>-final-captions.srt` | The approved reel and its captions |
| `assets/source/` | The real material the reel was built from: screenshots, screen recordings and demo clips of the product |
| `assets/graphics/` | The cropped and stitched images the motion graphics used (what `storyboard/assets/` held in the edit project) |

To re-edit one of these reels, copy `assets/source/` into `Raw/<Video>/Assets/` and `assets/graphics/` into the new project's `storyboard/assets/`. The raw camera footage and voiceovers are not in the repo.

**Left out for privacy:** two Muse screen recordings of a private agent chat and account settings, and the uncropped VeePN popup (it showed an IP address). The Muse landing-page recording has the browser's tab and bookmark bar cropped off. The client reels (Sarmila, Client IMG_2334) are not in the public repo.
